This repo houses gameplay network and also the gameplay data creation pipeline

The gameplay network is able to look at images from a video and classify whether the images represent
active volleyball gameplay or not.

From recent experiments, we know that a 4-frame temporal classifier works better than a single-frame
classifier. Our best network so far is tracked in model_registry.yaml. Training,
evaluation, and checkpoint-compatible model definitions live in the local
`skyball_gameplay` package.

Fresh-agent procedure: use workflows/new_dataset_static_val.md for the static
validation dataset refresh, then follow the user-requested training experiments.
